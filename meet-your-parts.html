Meet your parts

PartWhat it does here
Battery and holderSupply energy to the circuit.
Copper tapeMake conducting paths between parts.
LDR, the light sensorChange resistance as the light changes.
Sensor resistorWork with the LDR to create a changing control signal.
TransistorLet a small control signal control current through the LED branch.
LEDTransfer electrical energy into light and heat.
LED resistorLimit current through the LED.

The two resistors have different jobs. Keep both in the circuit shown by your teacher.
