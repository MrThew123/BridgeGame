LDR: Sensing point: LED current:

Teaching model: 6 V supply, example red LED (2 V), 470 Ω LED resistor, BC547 gain set to 200, base–emitter drop 0.65 V and minimum collector–emitter drop 0.2 V. The LDR varies from 1 kΩ to 1 MΩ; light-slider positions are illustrative, not lux measurements. The connected transistor loads the divider. Real components and switch-on points vary.

Based on the Techquity nightlight circuit. This revised model adds a 470 Ω LED current-limiting resistor, which is absent from the original template.
